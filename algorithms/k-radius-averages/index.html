K Radius Subarray Averages

For each index i, average the 2k + 1 numbers from i − k to i + k (integer division), or write -1 if that range runs off either end. Re-adding every window costs O(n·k). Instead, build a prefix-sum array once, where prefix[j] is the sum of the first j numbers. Then any window's sum is one subtraction: everything through its right end, minus everything before its left end.
